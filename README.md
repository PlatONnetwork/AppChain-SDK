# AppChain-SDK
[中文文档](./README_CN.md)

**AppChain-SDK** is a revolutionary, future-oriented, high-performance blockchain application chain framework. Its core mission is to break the shackles of existing blockchain development, providing developers with unprecedented flexibility and control.

The unique aspect of the AppChain-SDK framework lies in its **fully customizable, full-stack** architectural design. It can operate as a fully sovereign **Layer1** independent chain, function as a **Layer2** chain anchored to underlying public chains like Ethereum to share security, or even be tailored to create highly optimized **dedicated chains** for specific business scenarios.

**The design goal of AppChain-SDK** is to provide a rich and powerful set of extension interfaces for every core layer of the blockchain—including block, consensus, network, storage, transaction pool, virtual machine, and more. Developers no longer need to start from scratch; they can use AppChain-SDK to rapidly complete the development of a blockchain that meets their specific business needs and is highly customizable.

*   **Out-of-the-Box, Zero-Cost Startup**: AppChain-SDK comes pre-equipped with a wide range of battle-tested universal modules (such as PoS consensus, asset issuance, governance, etc.). Through simple modular combination, developers can achieve an initial chain setup with "zero cost," significantly lowering the barrier to entry.
*   **Seamless Integration with the Ethereum Ecosystem**: AppChain-SDK is fully compatible with the Ethereum protocol. This means existing Ethereum development tools (like MetaMask, Hardhat, Foundry, Truffle), smart contracts, and the vast DApp ecosystem can migrate to an AppChain-SDK chain almost seamlessly. This **greatly reduces the learning curve for developers**, allowing them to focus on business innovation rather than low-level adaptation.
